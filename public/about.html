How to Use LUKZI Downloader

Complete guide for downloading from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook on Android, iOS, and PC

1. YouTube Videos, Shorts & MP3

Download high-definition 1080p, 720p, 480p videos or convert to 320kbps MP3 audio seamlessly.

  1. Copy the URL of the YouTube video or Short from the YouTube app or browser.
  2. Paste the link into the search box on the homepage.
  3. Select MP4 Video (choose resolution) or MP3 Audio, then tap Download.

2. TikTok Videos (No Watermark)

Extract pure HD MP4 video streams without the floating watermark logo.

  1. Tap the "Share" button on TikTok and tap "Copy Link".
  2. Paste the link into LUKZI VS SOCIAL MEDIA.
  3. Click "Download TikTok (No Watermark)" to save directly to your gallery.

3. Instagram Reels & Videos

Download Instagram Reels and Videos in original studio quality with audio.

  1. Tap the three dots (⋯) or Share icon on the Instagram post and choose "Copy link".
  2. Paste the link into the downloader.
  3. Tap "Download Instagram HD Reel" to save it immediately.

4. Facebook Videos & Reels

Save public Facebook Watch videos and reels in crystal-clear HD or fast SD.

  1. Click "Share" on any public Facebook post and tap "Copy Link".
  2. Paste the link into LUKZI VS SOCIAL MEDIA.
  3. Click "Download Facebook HD Video".
