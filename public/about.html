How to Use LUKZI Downloader

Comprehensive guide for downloading social media media in full HD quality

YouTube & Shorts Video/Audio Download

LUKZI allows you to extract YouTube videos in multiple resolutions including 1080p, 720p HD, 480p, and MP3 high quality audio.

  1. Copy the URL of the YouTube video or Short from your browser or the YouTube mobile app.
  2. Paste the link into the downloader input on the homepage.
  3. Select MP4 Video (and choose resolution) or MP3 Audio, then click Download.

TikTok Video Download (No Watermark)

Download high-definition TikTok videos completely free of the bouncing watermark logo.

  1. Tap the "Share" icon on any TikTok video and tap "Copy Link".
  2. Paste the link into LUKZI VS SOCIAL MEDIA.
  3. Click "Download Video (No Watermark)" to save the pure MP4 video directly.
