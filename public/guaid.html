How to Use LUKZI VS SOCIAL MEDIA

Follow these 5 simple steps to download any video or audio directly to your device gallery or downloads folder in seconds.

Click on the platform button on the home page to select it.
Open your social media app or browser, find the video or reel.
Tap Share and select Copy Link.
Paste your link into the input box or click the clipboard paste button.
